About Voyager Academy

Voyager Academy started as a set of personal trading notes — the rules, mistakes, and hard-won lessons of actually trading forex — before it became a structured, free curriculum for anyone starting where we once did.

Why this exists

Most free trading content online is either a thinly-veiled pitch for paid signals, or so shallow it teaches you nothing you couldn't have guessed. We wanted something in between: real technical depth — liquidity theory, order blocks, Elliott Wave — taught plainly, with no upsell attached to finishing a lesson.

What makes it different

  • Written from direct trading experience, not licensed corporate content
  • Free, permanently — no lesson is gated behind a paywall
  • Beginner to advanced in one continuous path, plus a dedicated TradingView platform course
  • Connected to Voyager Analytics for when you're ready to apply what you've learned with a real trading journal and macro data

Who's behind it

Voyager Academy is built and maintained by a single South Africa-based trader and developer — the same person behind Voyager Analytics.
