Risk Disclaimer

Last updated · Print

Please read this: trading can lose you money — sometimes all of it, and with some products more than you put in. Nothing on Voyager Academy is advice. Only risk money you can afford to lose.

Educational content only

Voyager Academy teaches concepts and shows worked examples. It doesn't give personal recommendations, signals or advice, and it is no substitute for a licensed financial adviser. In South Africa, giving financial advice or acting as an intermediary is regulated under FAIS and overseen by the FSCA. Voyager Academy is not an authorised financial services provider and does not offer those services.

Trading is risky

Forex, CFDs, commodities, indices and other leveraged products can move fast. Leverage magnifies losses as well as gains, and with some products or brokers you can lose more than your deposit. Many regulators require providers to disclose that most retail clients lose money trading these products. Make sure you understand how they work and whether you can afford the risk.

Examples are not promises

Account sizes, win rates, R-multiples, worked examples and calculator results are illustrative or hypothetical. Hypothetical results have built-in limits: they don't reflect real spreads, slippage, emotions or discipline. Past performance — real or hypothetical — does not guarantee future results.

Calculators and tools

Our calculators use the numbers you enter and simplified assumptions, such as standard pip values. Always check results against your broker's contract specifications. We accept no responsibility for losses from relying on them.

Practise before you pay

Use a demo or paper-trading account to learn the mechanics first. Good demo results don't guarantee live results, because real money changes behaviour.

Funded-trader challenges

Challenges are paid evaluations. Fees are usually non-refundable, the rules are strict, and many attempts fail. "Funded" accounts may be simulated. Firms vary widely in reliability and some are scams, so read a firm's current terms and check for regulator warnings before paying. Our Proving Ground track is educational and is not a recommendation to buy any challenge.

Signals, copy trading and "guaranteed" returns

Be sceptical of anyone — including in our chat or forum — who promises guaranteed profits, sells signals, or asks you to send money or share broker logins. We don't endorse any such service.

Synthetic indices

Synthetic indices and similar products are generated by a provider's algorithm rather than by real-world supply and demand, so news and fundamental analysis don't apply to them in the same way. Learn how a product works before trading it.

Check your broker

Use only providers authorised by a regulator in your jurisdiction. In South Africa, you can check the FSCA's list of authorised financial services providers. Understand fees, spreads, leverage limits and withdrawal terms before you deposit.

Your responsibility

You alone are responsible for your trading decisions and their results. Consider your goals, finances and experience, and get independent advice if you're unsure. Never trade with money you need for living expenses, debts or emergencies.
