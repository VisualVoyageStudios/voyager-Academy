Trading Calculators

The formulas from the Risk Management lesson, ready to use. Everything runs in your browser — nothing is saved or sent anywhere.

Position Size Calculator

How many lots to trade so a stop-out costs exactly the amount you chose to risk.

The presets assume a USD account. "EUR/USD, GBP/USD" covers any pair where USD is the second currency (AUD/USD, NZD/USD and so on); "USD/CHF, USD/CAD" covers pairs where USD comes first. On a different account currency, or for gold, indices or crypto, choose "Other / custom" and enter the pip/point value from your broker's contract specifications, in the same currency as your balance. Always verify against your broker before trading.

Risk-to-Reward & Break-even Win Rate

Enter your planned trade to see the ratio — and how often you need to win for it to pay.

Drawdown Recovery

The bigger the loss, the disproportionately bigger the gain needed just to get back to even.

Educational tools only — not financial advice. Results depend on the numbers you enter and your broker's actual contract specifications.
