Privacy Policy

What we collect

When you create an account: your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (we never store your password in plain text). As you use the site, we record which lessons you've completed to track your progress and certificates.

How we use it

Solely to run your account, save your progress, and issue certificates. We don't sell your data to third parties.

Where it's stored

Your account data is stored in our database, hosted on our infrastructure provider.

Your rights

You can request access to or deletion of your data at any time by contacting us via the Community page.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time; the current version always applies.
