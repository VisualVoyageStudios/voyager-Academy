Privacy Policy

Last updated · Print

The short version: we collect only what we need to run your account and learning progress. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising. You can download or delete everything yourself in Settings.

Who we are

Voyager Academy is run by in South Africa, which is the "responsible party" under the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA). For any question or request about your information, contact .

What we collect

  • Account: your display name (and an optional separate name for certificates), your email address, and a one-way hash of your password — we never store the password itself. We also record when you joined.
  • Learning activity: which lessons you've completed and when. Streaks, badges and certificates are worked out from this.
  • Community: forum posts, replies and reports you make, and chat messages you send.
  • Settings: your notification preference and, if sync is on, your appearance and learning preferences.
  • Technical data: our servers record standard request details such as your IP address, browser type and the endpoints requested. We use these for security, rate limiting and troubleshooting.
  • Email: we send emails you trigger — password resets, and reply notifications if you leave them switched on.

We don't ask for payment details, ID numbers or broker account details, because nothing here is paid. Please don't share them in posts or chat.

What stays on your device

Some things are stored in your browser. Your login session is kept there and sent to our server with your requests so we know it's you. Your lesson notes, saved lessons, weak-spot practice data, last-viewed lesson and offline cache never leave your device. Your appearance settings stay on your device unless sync is on. Clearing your browser data removes all of it.

Why we use it

  • To create and run your account and provide lessons, quizzes, certificates, the community and chat — the service you asked for.
  • To keep things safe: preventing abuse, moderating content, rate limiting and investigating problems.
  • To send the service emails described above.
  • To understand which pages are used so we can improve the site (see the next section).

We don't use your information for advertising, and we don't sell it.

Analytics, cookies and third parties

We use your browser's local storage, not tracking cookies of our own, to keep you logged in and remember settings.

We use Google Analytics to see, in aggregate, which pages are visited and how people find the site. It may set cookies and receives your IP address and device and browser details. You can block it using your browser's privacy settings or an extension.

Charts, the economic calendar and the market ticker are loaded from TradingView's servers, which receive your IP address and may set their own cookies under their own privacy policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts.

Who we share it with

  • Service providers that process data on our behalf: GitHub (website hosting), Render (our API server), Neon (our database) and Brevo (email delivery).
  • Other students: your display name and anything you post. Forum posts may be visible to anyone who visits the site; chat is visible to logged-in students.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it or to protect people's rights and safety.

Some of these providers store or process data outside South Africa. Where that happens we rely on providers with appropriate safeguards, in line with POPIA. We don't sell or rent personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data, progress, posts and chat messages until you delete your account. When you delete it, they are removed from our live database straight away; copies may remain in provider backups for a short time before being overwritten. Server logs are kept for a limited period by our host.

Your rights

Under POPIA you can:

  • ask what we hold about you — Settings → Privacy & data → Download my data;
  • correct it — Settings → Account;
  • delete it — Settings → Security → Delete my account;
  • object to or limit some processing, or withdraw consent, by emailing us.

If you think we've handled your information badly, you can complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za. We'd appreciate the chance to put it right first.

Security

Passwords are stored as hashes, connections use HTTPS, sign-in and password-reset attempts are rate limited, and you can end sessions on all devices from Settings. No online service is completely secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

Children

Voyager Academy is for people aged 18 and over. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. If you think a child has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If our practices change we'll update this page and its date, and tell you on the site if the change is significant.
