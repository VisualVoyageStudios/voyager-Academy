Glossary

Every term introduced across the lessons, in one place.

Core Trading Terms

Pip
The standard small step in a pair's price: usually the fourth decimal place (0.0001), or the second (0.01) for yen pairs.
Lot
The size of a trade. A standard lot is 100,000 units, a mini lot 10,000 and a micro lot 1,000.
Spread
The gap between the bid and ask price. It's the cost you pay to enter a trade.
Margin
A deposit your broker holds while a trade is open. It's released when the trade closes.
Margin Call
A broker warning that your equity is too low to support your open trades. Positions may be closed automatically if it keeps falling.
Swap (Rollover)
Interest paid or earned for holding a position past the broker's daily cut-off.
Equity
Your account balance plus or minus the profit or loss on open trades.
Moving Average (SMA / EMA)
A line that smooths price over the last N candles. The EMA weights recent prices more than the SMA.
RSI
Relative Strength Index: a 0 to 100 momentum gauge. Readings above 70 or below 30 are labelled overbought or oversold, but they aren't signals on their own.
MACD
A momentum indicator built from the gap between a 12 and a 26 period EMA, with a 9 period signal line.
Bollinger Bands
Bands two standard deviations either side of a 20 period average. They widen when volatility rises.
ATR
Average True Range: the average size of recent candles, a measure of volatility.
Backtesting
Testing a set of trading rules on past price data to see how they would have performed.
Expectancy
The average result per trade, in R: win rate × average win minus loss rate × average loss.

Technical Analysis

Support / Resistance
Price zones where the market has repeatedly reacted — potential turning points.
Fibonacci Retracement
A tool that plots likely pullback levels (23.6%–88.6%) within a prior price swing.
BOS (Break of Structure)
A support or resistance level breaks, signaling a trend continuing or shifting.
CHoCH (Change of Character)
The market's behavior flips from bullish to bearish, or vice versa.
EQH / EQL (Equal Highs / Equal Lows)
Liquidity resting above a double top or below a double bottom.

Smart Money / ICT Concepts

Order Block
The last candle before a strong impulsive move — the market's "footprint."
Breaker Block
A failed order block that flips and acts as the opposite kind of zone.
Fair Value Gap (FVG)
An imbalance left when price moves so fast it skips a price range entirely.
IRL (Internal Range Liquidity)
FVGs, inverted FVGs, and order blocks sitting inside a range.
ERL (External Range Liquidity)
The swing highs and lows that bound a range.
DOL (Draw on Liquidity)
Where the market is being "pulled" toward — usually a liquidity pool.
CRT (Candle Range Theory)
A 3-candle model: liquidity made, purged, then neutralized.
Premium / Discount
The top half (sell zone) and bottom half (buy zone) of a price range.
Equilibrium
The exact midpoint of a range, separating premium from discount.
Consequent Encroachment (CE)
The 50% midpoint of a Fair Value Gap — often all price needs to reach before reacting.
BISI / SIBI
Formal ICT names for a bullish FVG (Buyside Imbalance, Sellside Inefficiency) and bearish FVG (reverse).

Platform & Account

Paper Trading
Risk-free simulated trading with virtual funds — no broker required.
Bracket Order
An order that sets entry, stop loss, and take profit together before it fills.
Bar Replay
Rewinds a chart to a past date and steps forward candle by candle for practice.
VWAP
Volume Weighted Average Price — the average price paid, weighted by volume, from a chosen point.
Trailing Stop
A stop loss that automatically moves as price moves in your favor.
COT Report
A weekly CFTC report showing how large speculators, commercials, and small traders are positioned.

Prop firm

R (R-multiple)
Your per-trade risk used as a unit: a stop-out is −1R, a trade that makes twice its risk is +2R.
Open Risk
What you'd lose if every currently open trade hit its stop at once.
Circuit Breaker
A pre-set rule that forces a pause or smaller size after a losing run.
Trailing Drawdown
A loss limit measured from your account's peak rather than its starting balance, so it rises as you profit.
Funded Challenge
A paid evaluation where you trade under a firm's rules and, if you pass, receive a funded account with a profit split.
