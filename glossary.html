Glossary

Every term introduced across the 19 lessons, in one place.

Core Trading Terms

Pip
The smallest standard price movement in a pair — usually the fourth decimal place.
Lot
A standardized trade size: standard = 100,000 units, mini = 10,000, micro = 1,000.
Spread
The gap between the buy (ask) and sell (bid) price — the built-in cost of entering a trade.
Leverage
Lets a smaller amount of capital control a larger position — amplifies gains and losses equally.
Drawdown
How far an account has fallen from a prior peak, usually shown as a percentage.
Risk-to-Reward Ratio
How much you stand to make versus how much you're risking on a trade (e.g., 1:3).
Expectancy
(Win Rate × Average Win) − (Loss Rate × Average Loss) — the number that actually predicts long-run profitability.

Technical Analysis

Support / Resistance
Price zones where the market has repeatedly reacted — potential turning points.
Fibonacci Retracement
A tool that plots likely pullback levels (23.6%–88.6%) within a prior price swing.
BOS (Break of Structure)
A support or resistance level breaks, signaling a trend continuing or shifting.
CHoCH (Change of Character)
The market's behavior flips from bullish to bearish, or vice versa.
EQH / EQL (Equal Highs / Equal Lows)
Liquidity resting above a double top or below a double bottom.

Smart Money / ICT Concepts

Order Block
The last candle before a strong impulsive move — the market's "footprint."
Breaker Block
A failed order block that flips and acts as the opposite kind of zone.
Fair Value Gap (FVG)
An imbalance left when price moves so fast it skips a price range entirely.
IRL (Internal Range Liquidity)
FVGs, inverted FVGs, and order blocks sitting inside a range.
ERL (External Range Liquidity)
The swing highs and lows that bound a range.
DOL (Draw on Liquidity)
Where the market is being "pulled" toward — usually a liquidity pool.
CRT (Candle Range Theory)
A 3-candle model: liquidity made, purged, then neutralized.
Premium / Discount
The top half (sell zone) and bottom half (buy zone) of a price range.
Equilibrium
The exact midpoint of a range, separating premium from discount.
Consequent Encroachment (CE)
The 50% midpoint of a Fair Value Gap — often all price needs to reach before reacting.
BISI / SIBI
Formal ICT names for a bullish FVG (Buyside Imbalance, Sellside Inefficiency) and bearish FVG (reverse).

Platform & Account

Paper Trading
Risk-free simulated trading with virtual funds — no broker required.
Bracket Order
An order that sets entry, stop loss, and take profit together before it fills.
Bar Replay
Rewinds a chart to a past date and steps forward candle by candle for practice.
VWAP
Volume Weighted Average Price — the average price paid, weighted by volume, from a chosen point.
Trailing Stop
A stop loss that automatically moves as price moves in your favor.
COT Report
A weekly CFTC report showing how large speculators, commercials, and small traders are positioned.
